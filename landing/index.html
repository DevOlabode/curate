Browser extension

Keep the links you actually return to.

Curate is a private library in your toolbar. Save docs, repos, tools, and articles from a popup, file them into collections, and find them again without hunting through a bookmarks bar.

What it is

Bookmark bars fill up and then get ignored. Social bookmarking tools ask you to publish. Curate is neither. It is a small, quiet place for the development links you keep reopening.

You sign in inside the popup. You save a URL with a title, a category, and tags. You can leave it in the main library or drop it into a collection. Next time the popup opens, that library is the first thing you see.

What it includes

  1. 01

    Bookmarks

    Save a title, URL, category, and tags from the popup. Open the link later in one click. Edit or delete it without leaving the extension.

  2. 02

    Collections

    Group related bookmarks into named sets: a stack you are learning, a project you are shipping, a topic you refuse to lose. Open a collection and add straight into it.

  3. 03

    Accounts in the popup

    Create an account or sign in from the extension. Edit your profile, change your password, log out, or delete your account there. This website is not where you manage your library.

  4. 04

    Private by default

    Links and notes stay on your account. There is no public feed, no sharing wall, and no one else browsing what you saved.

  5. 05

    Light and dark

    The popup stays readable in both modes, so it works at a desk or late at night without becoming a different product.

  6. 06

    Library first

    The home screen is your collections and bookmarks. Forms stay hidden until you add or edit something.

Install

On Chrome and Edge.

Get Curate from the Chrome Web Store or Microsoft Edge Add-ons. There is no web signup on this site. Sign in inside the popup.
